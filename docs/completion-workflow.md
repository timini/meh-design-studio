# Completion target: inexpensive multi-driver freeform waveguide

The maintainer clarified on 10 September 2026 that the product must optimise a
shared MEH/waveguide using multiple inexpensive drivers. A conical mechanical
prototype does not meet that goal. Solana is the functional reference for smooth
coverage and coherent integration, not a shape to copy or a claimed equivalent.
[JW Sound describes genetic optimisation of its profile and port diffraction](https://www.jwsound.live/designs/solana-diy).

The implementation must connect:

1. A shared geometric model of the horn air, entry ducts, front cavities, rear
   loads and build solids. Support a four-driver ring and variable nonconical,
   noncircular profiles; mutations must change the mesh actually solved.
2. Coupled electrical/mechanical drivers and three-dimensional acoustics with
   exterior radiation. Retain every driver's induced motion and the complex
   source-to-observation transfer basis.
3. Reproducible simulation-driven shape mutation and selection. Preserve budgets,
   failed trials, parentage and exact inputs. Optimise response, horizontal/
   vertical directivity and whole-sphere behaviour for irregular shapes together;
   freeze DSP for independent finalist checks.
4. Numerical references, sampled-frequency holdouts, mesh convergence and
   baseline/random-search comparisons with unchanged declared limits.
5. A traceable driver BOM, drive settings and geometry exports from the actual
   selected candidate. Manufacturing interfaces must correspond to the model.
6. Commercial source data with provenance and uncertainty, followed by physical
   source/load and assembled-speaker measurements. Synthetic circuit fixtures
   establish software/numerical behaviour only.

The updated approximately 350 Hz handover, wide vocal midrange and GBP 300
per-speaker budget remain the practical design challenge. The provisional ±3 dB screening band is
not an achieved result; directivity and output criteria need explicit reporting.
No software-only result completes measurement, distortion, airtightness or print
qualification. These requirements remain open until their evidence exists.

The shared ring topology, freeform geometry and adaptive search have now executed
native experiments. The first target-band search still misses its acoustic target.
Preserve the historical R1 diagnostic failures; do not relabel
R1's exports or prescribed-flow results as a new coupled optimised design.

After completing the design and its evidence, the maintainer has requested and
explicitly authorised a technical forum post to the DIY community used by JW
Sound. The destination is a new diyAudio project thread. Prepare an evidence-led
write-up, credit prior work, distinguish prediction from measurement, and link
reproducible project/design artifacts. Do not publish a premature success claim.

## Updated practical target

The latest maintainer instruction supersedes R1's original 240 Hz lower target:
use approximately 350 Hz to meet an X1 sub/HD15 kick system, prioritising a
continuous wide vocal midrange and a higher HF crossover (explore up to roughly
5 kHz, subject to directivity, source validity and breakup constraints). The
Funktion-One wide-mid approach is a functional reference; its Evolution X lists
280 Hz–5 kHz for the mid band: https://funktion-one.com/product/evolution-x/.
Do not assume an unmeasured HD15 assembly remains usable at 350 Hz.

The objective is to rival Solana 2.0's relevant mid/high performance for a fraction
of its price; this is a target requiring comparable measurements, not an existing
claim. Keep GBP 300 per speaker as the current cost ceiling until superseded.
The maintainer's amplifier supports 2-ohm loads. Optimise a single shared mid
amplifier bank, including full parallel wiring where the predicted impedance and
current remain compatible; preserve common voltage, sum all currents and model
mutual loading. Nominal driver impedance alone is insufficient for that check.

Amplifier preference: an inexpensive Sinbosen plate/DSP module. Select an exact
module only after checking its per-channel 2-ohm rating, voltage/current limits,
DSP capability, cooling and price; rack amplifier ratings are not transferable.
